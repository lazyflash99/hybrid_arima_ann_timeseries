# Datasets

All 11 series load offline. Five are CSV files in `data/`, three are bundled with the statsmodels package, and three are generated by `data_loader.py` with a fixed seed. `data_loader.DATASETS` holds the registry below.

| Name | Stored in | Source | Frequency | Length | Train / test | Transformation | Season period |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sunspot | bundled with statsmodels (`sm.datasets.sunspots`) | statsmodels sunspots (yearly, cut to 1700-1987) | yearly | 288 | 221 / 67 | none | 11 |
| Lynx | `data/lynx.csv` | R lynx data set (data/lynx.csv) | yearly | 114 | 100 / 14 | log10 | 10 |
| Exchange rate | `data/exchange_rate.csv` | FRED DEXUSUK, Wednesdays | weekly | 731 | 679 / 52 | log10 | 1 |
| AirPassengers | `data/airpassengers.csv` | R AirPassengers (data/airpassengers.csv) | monthly | 144 | 120 / 24 | log10 | 12 |
| Nile | bundled with statsmodels (`sm.datasets.nile`) | statsmodels nile | yearly | 100 | 80 / 20 | none | 1 |
| Daily min temp | `data/daily_min_temperatures.csv` | Melbourne daily minimum temperature, last 1095 days (github.com/jbrownlee/Datasets) | daily | 1095 | 915 / 180 | none | 365 |
| FX JPY | `data/fred_dexjpus.csv` | FRED DEXJPUS, Wednesdays | weekly | 731 | 679 / 52 | log10 | 1 |
| Sunspot sqrt | bundled with statsmodels (square root taken in `data_loader.py`) | statsmodels sunspots, square root | yearly | 288 | 221 / 67 | sqrt | 11 |
| Synthetic additive | generated in `data_loader.py` (seed 123) | generated: AR(2) + 3 sin(y_{t-1}) | synthetic | 400 | 320 / 80 | none | 1 |
| Synthetic multiplicative | generated in `data_loader.py` (seed 124) | generated: L_t x (1 + 0.4 sin(2 L_{t-1})) | synthetic | 400 | 320 / 80 | none | 1 |
| Mackey-Glass | generated in `data_loader.py` (seed 125) | generated: Mackey-Glass, tau = 17 | synthetic | 500 | 400 / 100 | none | 1 |

Generated by `data_loader.write_datasets_md`.
